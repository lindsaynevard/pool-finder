Swim For Fitness

  • Lanes are available with emphasis on sharing with swimmers of equal ability and circle swimming.

  • Use of the pool is not exclusive and will be shared with other CSD or EUSD programs.

  • Days and times listed below are for lap swimming.

  • Swim equipment is available (i.e., kickboards, pull buoys, deck chairs, loungers, etc.) on a first come first serve basis. You may bring your own swim equipment.

  • Swim For Fitness is designed for Adults and youth 14+.

  • Swimmers ages 10 - 13 may only participate in the water accompanied by an adult at all times.

  • Swimmers ages 14-17 must have an adult sign the waiver for them in person before participating in the water.

  • Learn more about Lap Swim Etiquette(PDF, 108KB), detailing program policies and expectations.

Swim Attire Policy

Our swim attire policy has been established to provide all swimmers with a safe, comfortable, and inclusive experience in our programs. We respectfully ask all swimmers to cooperate with the following swim attire guidelines.

Encouraged swimming attire consists of, but not limited to - swim trunks, swimsuits, athletic apparel, jammers, swim shirt/rash guards, wetsuits, and swim diapers.

Prohibited attire for swimming - denim, undergarments, excessively baggy, or soiled clothing. Clothing with metal features such as zippers, rivets, and chains is prohibited. If you require special accommodation, please contact aquatics staff.
